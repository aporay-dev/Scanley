❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the text recognition?

Scanley uses advanced OCR technology that works well with printed text, typed documents, and clear handwriting. For best results, ensure good lighting and steady hands when taking photos.

Where are my documents stored?

All your documents and extracted text are stored locally on your device for maximum privacy. Nothing is sent to the cloud unless you choose to share specific documents.

What types of documents work best?

Scanley works with receipts, invoices, contracts, business cards, handwritten notes, printed documents, forms, and more. Any document with readable text will work.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime through your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Scanley. Your Pro features will remain active until the current billing period ends.

Can I try Pro features for free?

Yes! All Pro subscriptions include a 7-day free trial. You can try unlimited searches, all categories, and full-screen viewing risk-free.
