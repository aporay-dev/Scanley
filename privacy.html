Scanley Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2025

🔒 Privacy First Approach

Your documents never leave your device. Scanley is designed with privacy at its core - all scanning, text recognition, and document storage happens locally on your iPhone or iPad.

1. Information We Collect

Information We DON'T Collect

  • Document Content: We never see, access, or store your scanned documents
  • Extracted Text: All OCR text recognition happens on your device
  • Personal Information: No account creation required
  • Location Data: We don't track your location
  • Photos: Your camera photos are processed locally and not transmitted

Information We May Collect

  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous app usage statistics (crashes, feature usage)
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status for Pro features (handled by Apple)
  • Device Information: iOS version, device model for compatibility purposes

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Local Storage Only

All your documents, extracted text, and app data are stored exclusively on your device using iOS's secure storage systems. This means:

  • Your documents are protected by your device's encryption
  • Data is only accessible through the Scanley app
  • If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed
  • No cloud storage means no risk of data breaches

3. Data Processing

Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

When you scan a document:

  • The photo is processed locally on your device
  • Text recognition happens using on-device AI technology
  • Extracted text is stored locally in the app's secure database
  • No images or text are sent to external servers

Search and Organization

Document search and categorization features:

  • All search operations happen locally on your device
  • Document categorization uses on-device intelligence
  • No search queries or results are transmitted externally

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Scanley Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store:

  • Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple
  • We receive only subscription status information from Apple
  • No payment card details are stored or accessed by Scanley
  • Subscription management is done through your Apple ID settings

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

To improve the app, we may collect anonymous usage data:

  • App Performance: Crash reports and performance metrics
  • Feature Usage: Which features are used most (no personal data)
  • Device Information: iOS version and device type for compatibility

Note: All analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. We cannot identify individual users or their specific documents.

6. Data Sharing

We Do NOT Share Your Data

Scanley does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Since your documents are stored locally, there's nothing to share even if we wanted to.

The only data sharing occurs when:

  • You explicitly share: When you use iOS share features to send a document
  • Apple services: Anonymous analytics data may be shared with Apple for App Store optimization

7. Data Retention

  • Document Data: Stored on your device until you delete the app or individual documents
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized data may be retained for up to 2 years for improvement purposes
  • Subscription Data: Managed by Apple according to their privacy policy

8. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete Documents: Remove individual documents anytime within the app
  • Delete All Data: Delete the app to remove all stored data
  • Disable Analytics: Contact us to opt out of anonymous usage analytics
  • Subscription Control: Manage through iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

9. Security

Your data security is protected by:

  • iOS Security: All data benefits from iOS's built-in encryption
  • App Sandboxing: Data is isolated from other apps
  • No Network Transmission: Documents never leave your device
  • Secure Storage: Uses iOS secure storage APIs

10. Children's Privacy

Scanley is safe for users of all ages:

  • No personal information collection
  • No online features or chat
  • All processing happens locally
  • COPPA compliant due to privacy-first design

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify users of significant changes through app updates
  • Maintain the privacy-first approach in any future versions

12. Contact Information

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data:

Email: privacy@scanley.app

Support: Visit our support page

13. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Union, our legal basis for processing is:

  • Legitimate Interest: Providing core app functionality
  • Consent: Optional analytics and improvement features
  • Contract: Subscription service provision
